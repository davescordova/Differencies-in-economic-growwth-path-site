About

In this role, I apply my technical expertise to complex audits and electoral account analyses, ensuring compliance and the proper allocation of public resources.

Data analysis led me to develop advanced skills in quantitative and econometric tools, utilizing programming languages such as Python and Julia to model and interpret economic scenarios, with a special focus on economic growth, development, and public finance.
