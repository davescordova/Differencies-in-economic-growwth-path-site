About Me

With a multidisciplinary academic and professional background, I am a public sector economics specialist driven by the challenge of promoting efficiency, economic growth, and transparency in public governance. I hold a Master's degree in Economics from the Federal University of Mato Grosso (UFMT), a Bachelor's degree in Economic Sciences (UFMT), and a Bachelor's degree in History from São Paulo State University (Unesp).

Currently, I serve as an External Public Auditor at the Court of Accounts of Mato Grosso (TCE-MT), on temporary assignment to the Regional Electoral Court (TRE-MT). In this role, I apply technical expertise to complex audits and electoral account analyses, ensuring compliance and the optimal allocation of public resources.

My work extensively relies on quantitative methods and spatial econometrics, utilizing programming languages such as Python and Julia to model economic scenarios, analyze regional development, and evaluate public policies.
